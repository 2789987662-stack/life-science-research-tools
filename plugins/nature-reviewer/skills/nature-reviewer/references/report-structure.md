3. Major strengths

2. Consensus blocking concerns
3. Other consensus major concerns
4. Differences in emphasis or judgment
5. Minor revision checklist
6. Broad-interest / significance readout
7. Most important issues to resolve
8. Unsupported / not-assessable claims
